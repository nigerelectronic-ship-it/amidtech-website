Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · AmidTech LLC

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for services and products purchased from AmidTech LLC. It forms part of our Terms of Service; a signed agreement may set specific terms, which then prevail.

Summary. Cancel before work starts: deposit refunded (minus any non-refundable third-party costs). After work starts: you pay only for work performed. After delivery and acceptance: no refund, but defects are fixed free of charge for 12 months.

1. Software, websites and applications

  • Before work starts: if you cancel in writing before the project kick-off, the deposit is refunded in full, less any non-refundable third-party costs already incurred on your behalf (e.g. domain names, licences, hosting).
  • During the project: if you cancel after work has started, AmidTech invoices the work performed up to the cancellation date, pro rata to the project milestones; any amount paid above that is refunded.
  • After delivery and acceptance: fees are non-refundable. Defects are corrected free of charge under the 12-month warranty.
  • If AmidTech fails to deliver the agreed scope and cannot remedy it within 30 days of written notice, the Client is entitled to a refund of the amounts paid for the undelivered part.

2. IT equipment

  • Orders can be cancelled free of charge before the equipment is ordered from the supplier.
  • Unopened equipment may be returned within 14 days of delivery in its original packaging; return shipping and any restocking fee charged by the supplier are borne by the Client.
  • Defective equipment is repaired or replaced under the manufacturer's warranty.

3. Maintenance and hosting plans

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month. Months already started are not refunded.

4. Consulting

Consulting days can be rescheduled free of charge with at least 48 hours' notice. Days cancelled with less notice may be invoiced.

5. How to request a cancellation or refund

Write to contact@amidtechgroup.com with your invoice number and the reason. We acknowledge within 2 business days and decide within 10 business days. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method (card or bank account) within 10 business days; bank or card processing times may add a few days.

6. Disputes

Please contact us before opening a dispute with your bank or card issuer; most issues can be resolved quickly by e-mail.
