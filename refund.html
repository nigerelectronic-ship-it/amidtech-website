Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

This policy applies to all services and products sold by AmidTech LLC. Our goal is to resolve every issue fairly and quickly.

1. Software, application and website projects

  • Before work starts: if the Client cancels within 7 days of paying the deposit and before work has begun, the deposit is refunded in full.
  • After work starts: the deposit covers work performed and is non-refundable; if the work performed is worth less than the deposit, the difference is refunded. Completed milestones are invoiced at their agreed value.
  • Non-conformity: if a deliverable does not meet the agreed specifications and we are unable to correct it within a reasonable time, the Client is entitled to a refund of the amounts paid for the non-conforming part.

2. Maintenance subscriptions

Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time with effect at the end of the current billing month. The current month is not prorated.

3. Consulting

Consulting days can be rescheduled free of charge with at least 48 hours' notice. Days cancelled with less notice may be invoiced.

4. IT hardware

  • Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are placed with the supplier.
  • Unopened items may be returned within 14 days of delivery for a refund, less return shipping and any supplier restocking fee.
  • Defective items are repaired or replaced under the manufacturer's warranty; if neither is possible, the item is refunded.

5. How to request a cancellation or refund

Write to contact@amidtechgroup.com with your invoice number and the reason. We acknowledge within 2 business days and decide within 10 business days. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method (card or bank account) within 10 business days; bank or card processing times may add a few days.

6. Disputes

Please contact us before opening a dispute with your bank or card issuer; most issues can be resolved quickly by e-mail.
