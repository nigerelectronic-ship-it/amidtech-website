Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AmidTech LLC collects and uses personal information from clients, prospects and website visitors.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you send us: name, company, e-mail, phone, message content.
  • Billing information: company name, address, tax identifiers, invoice and payment records.
  • Project data you entrust to us to build or maintain your software.
  • Basic technical data from our hosting provider (server logs, IP address) for security.

Card payments are processed by our third-party payment processor; we do not receive or store full card numbers.

2. How we use it

To answer requests, prepare quotes, perform contracts, invoice and collect payments, provide support, meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, and secure our services. We do not sell personal information and do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Sharing

We share information only with service providers needed to run our business (hosting, e-mail, banking and payment processing, accounting), with authorities when required by law, or with your consent.

Website assistant and news bar

The chat assistant on our website answers questions about our services. Messages typed in the assistant are sent to our AI service provider (Anthropic, PBC) only to generate a reply; they are not used for advertising and are not stored by AmidTech beyond the conversation. Please do not share passwords, card numbers or identity documents in the chat. The news bar shows public headlines with links to the original publishers; we are not responsible for third-party content.

4. Cookies

Our website uses no advertising or tracking cookies. Your language choice may be stored locally in your browser.

5. Retention

Billing and contract records are kept as long as required by tax and accounting law (generally 7 years). Other data is kept for the duration of the relationship and deleted or returned on request when no longer needed.

6. Security

We use HTTPS encryption, access controls and reputable providers to protect your information.

7. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal information by writing to contact@amidtechgroup.com. We respond within 30 days.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version on this page.
